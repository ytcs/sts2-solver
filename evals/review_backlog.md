- **RL curriculum design for the solver** (agreed with the user, 2026-10-06):
  - Everything in absolute HP; real starting HP with a 99-turn cap (no 999 HP: HP-conditional cards and relics need no special case).
  - Value head decomposed into outcomes: P(win), HP lost if won (distribution), potions used (per potion). The decision layer combines them with run-specific weights (win / loss stakes from the route, a price per potion from its value in the next boss fight); no reward weights baked into the network.
  - Difficulty is adaptive and used for SAMPLING only (prioritized replay of fights the solver wins 20-80 %), not in the reward.
  - Curriculum: start with easy fights, no potions, no extra relics, starter-like decks (a few cards added / removed / transformed, like Neow), high starting HP; then harder fights, potions up to 8, more relics, larger and more varied decks (upgrades, curses, colorless, other characters' cards, event cards), ancient relics. - **Common random numbers: coupling is weak for card and potion variants** (2026-10-06, commits 5954d81, 3918194). Play-outs are now seeded by job and variants of one encounter share job seeds; copies come out identical. Paired se vs the independent formula: upgrade 2.0x smaller, removal 1.3x, added card 1.0x, boss belt variants 1.0-1.7x (4.5x for a potion rarely thrown). Likely causes: (1) the belt and deck are in the observation, so the policy's probabilities shift from turn 1 and the shared uniform picks other moves; (2) the root's future keys (`Block::rng`, sequential) desync whenever one variant searches a decision the other plays at once; (3) the fight-start shuffle depends on deck size. Candidates, each measured with `tools/crn_check.py` / `paired_real.py`-style variants before building: future keys derived from (job seed, turn, decision index) instead of a sequential stream; an eval-only shuffle by per-card-instance random keys (same distribution, live play untouched). Payoff: task A (per-potion pricing) needs ~128 attempts per potion at se ~0.05 today.
