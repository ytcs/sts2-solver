Normalise training by starting the player at a fixed large HP (e.g. 999) whatever the character, so the value is "HP lost in this fight" with no death truncation; the run-level worth of that loss (death at the real HP, the HP-worth curve) is applied outside the network at decision time. Questions: interaction with HP-conditional effects (Meat on the Bone, Blood Wall, low-HP cards), and the terminal for an actual death.
