# Deck building: the numbers are one input; the decision weighs them with what they cannot see

**The numbers `[sim]`:** every option priced on the SMOOTH objective (win rate averaged over start HP x1 / 1.5 / 2 / 3 of the real HP). As a stand-alone pick rule (best smooth, never skip unless every option is worse) it beat "best win rate now, skip when no gain" 0.70 vs 0.31 (Lagavulin) and 0.18 vs 0.00 (Aeonglass); lookahead variants +20-30%, not significant at 6 sequences (`evidence.md`). It is greedy, additive and combat-only, so it is one input, not the decision: the decision weighs it with the buckets, the weakest fight, density and the future problems (sections 2-6). `[expert]` (JapaneseExport, youtube IqNkmsA0PLo): picks that look bad now are made for a plan.

## 1. Protocol (every card reward, shop item, relic, upgrade, removal, transform)
1. Buckets and fights first (before any number): `brief` gives the five-bucket line and gaps (`data/card_buckets_ironclad.json`). Name the WEAKEST upcoming fight (the boss, or the elite with the lowest win; `reward` prints it), what it asks (`sts2-acts/encounters.md`, `sts2-mechanics`: front damage, scaling, block pattern, a condition) and which bucket answers that; write the plan card(s) of the target deck (section 4). A fight the deck already wins (>= ~0.95) is solved: it does not decide the pick.
2. Numbers. Card reward: `reward [--attempts N] [--hp full]` (every option and skip against the boss smooth, elites left and next act, the buckets each card fills, the weakest fight and the need-weighted gain; a card with no simulator id is "not evaluated": use `eval`). Else `eval --smooth --boss|--elites|--next --v "name|add=ID|remove=ID|upgrade=ID|relics_add=ID"` (removals, upgrades, relics, potions, transforms are variants). Read the need-weighted gain and the weakest-fight column, not only the mean: the mean rewards polishing fights that are already won.
3. Judgment: weigh the table with the rest. Read the `section-3 bar` lines `reward` prints (a card into solved buckets needs boss smooth > max(3 se, 0.05) or +0.10 on the weakest fight, else skip is the default), walk section 2 (density, enablers, interim survival, ...) and the buckets: does the pick answer the weakest fight's bucket or a future problem, add a card the plan needs or a ninth filler, open a synergy.
4. Act in one call with the decision record: `a <i> -- buckets: <line, open ones>; weakest: <fight, what it asks>; numbers: <best +x, the chosen card's gain and bar status>; judgment: <what decided it: plan fit, density, future problem, synergy; blind-spot numbers>` (`<i>` = option index, or `a ~<card name>`). The harness refuses a card pick without this screen's `reward` table or without the four fields.

Picky or not: `pickplan [--screens K] [--shops S]` (about 3 min) prices every pool card for the current deck and finds the take-threshold tau* for the offers still to come before the dangerous fight (optimal stopping with a slot limit; pools and odds are `[code]`, rho and slots `[hyp]`). The threshold falls with the runway (K = 0: take anything positive) and is small next to a card that fills the weakest fight's need: skip only near-zero picks. Time budget: one `reward` per screen (~35 s), `pickplan` once per act and after each shop, no extra evals unless two options are within 2 se.

Under 2 se (~0.05 at 96 attempts) is a tie: break it by bucket gap and plan fit. Value is not additive (Blood Wall -5 -> +10 once Inferno was in): re-price after every pick.

**Saturated smooth score: judge at the real HP.** When the smooth columns at x1.5 and above are >= ~0.9, the average hides what an option does at the HP I will fight at: re-price the close options with a plain `eval --boss --hp <arrival HP> --attempts 192+` and decide on that column. `[sim]` Rupture+ vs Test Subject + Aeonglass: smooth +0.023 (se 0.021, a tie) but +0.181 (se 0.030) at the real 72 HP.

**Screens that allow several picks (Orrery, multi-set rewards, a shop):** price bundles, not only single cards. The question is which SET of picks to take: evaluate the best candidate of each offer together (`--v "bundle|add=A,B,C"`) and the leave-one-out bundles, at the real HP, against the deck without them. Several individual ties can add up to a clear gain. Never skip every offer of a multi-pick screen on single-card ties. `[hyp]` Test: bundle vs skip-all at the real HP the next time.

## 2. Blind spots of the numbers (rule | test | status)
1. **Enablers and payoffs dead until the partner arrives** (Barricade, Demon Form with few hits, Feel No Pain without exhaust, Dark Embrace, Corruption). Take the enabler only if the bundle gain is large AND >= 2 payoffs are in the deck or likely met (known shop / ancient rares, elite rewards) AND the interim cost is survivable; else skip. Test: `--v "enabler|add=E" --v "bundle|add=E,P1,P2" --v "payoff alone|add=P1,P2"`; `route` the next fights with the enabler as a dead card. `[hyp]`
2. **Offer availability is the bottleneck:** best 20-card deck of any cards 0.62 vs best online deck 0.25. Buy access (elites, shops with gold kept for Act 2-3, ancients / Neow, card-choice events, boss potions): a +4 pick is worth less than a route adding an elite or shop before the boss. Test: `route` variants. `[sim]`
3. **Rares and relics are targets, not increments.** Build the target deck (section 4) and find the 2-3 cards that make it exist (Demon Form +12/+9 beat Tear Asunder +22 elites, +0 boss). Test: boss-alone `eval`. `[hyp]`
4. **Interim survival.** The boss smooth score ignores the hallways and elites before the payoff; a "worse now" pick is valid only if it still reaches the boss gate HP. Test: `route` the next fights, `eval --boss --hp <arrival>`. `[hyp]`
5. **Draw dependence.** Prefer picks that survive a bad draw (acceleration, cheap cards, redundancy) over plans needing a specific draw. Test: plan failed after turn 1-2 draws: switch (section 5). `[hyp]`
6. **Engines the network may undervalue** (exhaust, Primal Force + Demon Form, 5-7 energy; high energy itself showed no policy gap `[sim]`). Test: theory says high ceiling but `eval` says ~0: more attempts, one fight with `adv 20`, `eval` variants adding Strength / Block / energy. `[hyp]`
7. **Deck size and density.** Each pick is priced against the deck it joins, so ties and +0.03 picks pile up and later cost more than they gave. `[sim]` (a 28-card Act 2 deck vs Kaiser Crab, 96 attempts, se ~0.013): removing its 8 starter Strikes / Defends +0.20 smooth, its 5 filler picks +0.05, while its bucket counts showed no gap: counts do not measure density. Rules: (a) skip a pick that is within 2 se of skip once the deck passes ~20 cards; (b) every few picks run `rmcalc` and a bundle `remove=` variant of the weakest cards, not one at a time (one removal is ~+0.03, eight are +0.20); (c) buy removals before marginal cards (a shop's removal price rises per use); (d) the key cards (the 3-4 that carry the boss plan) need a low-dilution deck. Test: `eval --smooth --boss --v "thin|remove=A,B,C"` against the deck as is. `[sim]` one deck; `[hyp]` that the pick rule (never skip unless every option is worse) should become "skip ties past ~20 cards" (test: two runs, 22-card vs 28-card).
8. **Pickup and economy effects** (gold, max HP, pickup relics, events, shop prices, potion slots, Neow trades, curses taken for relics) are not in `eval` (curses: `add=CURSE_ID`). Test: price in HP / gold, compare with the combat delta. `[code]`
9a. **Ancient / event options that enchant a card** (Imbued: a Skill auto-played free on turn 1) are priced like any card: `eval --v "x|enchant=FLAME_BARRIER:IMBUED"` (ID:ENCHANTMENT, enchantment ids from `sts2.names()`). Against a boss that is a cliff in start HP (Kaiser Crab: x1.5 column swings 0.1 to 0.4 between near-identical decks) the smooth score's se understates the noise: re-price a close pair with `--seed 1` (a plain re-run repeats the same draws) or 2x attempts before choosing. `[sim]`
9b. **Price the elites at the HP I will arrive with, not at full HP.** At full HP the elite column saturates (0.97 for most picks), so a pick that decides an elite at the HP the route delivers looks worthless. Use `reward --hp <q50 elite arrival from routes>` or `eval --elites --hp <arrival>` whenever an elite is on the planned route. `[sim]`
9. **Fight-specific rules the bucket view hides** (Slippery, Artifact, Thorns, Hard to Kill, Withers: `sts2-mechanics`). Test: boss-alone `eval`, never the pool average. `[code]`
10. **Time horizon.** Score carry-over (scaling, energy, draw, rares, relics, boss potions, upgrades). Once the current boss is above ~85% (or after the Act 1 boss) later horizons decide: +3 now and +12 later beats +8 now and -3 later. Test: `reward` next-act columns, `eval --future`. `[hyp]`

## 3. The five buckets
- FD front-loaded damage, SD scaling damage (Strength, Inferno, Rampage), FB front-loaded block, SB scaling block (Dexterity, Feel No Pain, Metallicize), ACC acceleration (energy, draw, cycling; Barricade belongs here: it saves block, it does not add it). A card can sit in several; one in none is a candidate to skip.
- **A solved bucket sets a very high bar.** A bucket is solved when the fights ahead (this act's boss and elites, the next act's) no longer ask for more of it: its count is at the level they need and no gap is printed, judged at the HP I will arrive with (blind spot 9b), not at full HP. A card that only adds to a solved bucket must clearly beat skip (well beyond 2 se, or a large gain on the weakest fight) to be taken: a small + buys polish and costs density (blind spot 7). Between tied cards of a solved bucket the default is skip, not "which helps more". Spend picks on an open bucket, on the known weakness of a coming fight (`encounters.md`), or on a new synergy / engine piece (blind spots 1, 3, 6, 10), even when that card prices worse NOW: say which future problem it solves in the `-- why`. `[hyp]` Test: per run, picks into solved buckets vs the deck's later density (`rmcalc`) and the next-act column.
- Write the five-number line after every pick (`brief` prints it). Prefer the pick that raises the lowest bucket an upcoming fight asks about, unless the numbers show a large gain elsewhere. `[hyp]` Test: in `review`, the lowest bucket at each lost fight vs what that fight asks.

## 4. What each fight asks and the target deck
- Order: the act's boss, the elites that can still appear (`sts2-acts` bag), then hallways. Per fight: front-damage need, scaling need (long fight, enrage), block pattern (huge hit every N turns vs many small), conditions. Sources: `encounters.md`, `sts2-mechanics`, the enemy schedule (pass turns with a huge-HP player, read the intents).
- Target decks per later boss live in `encounters.md`; check every reward, shop and event against them first.
- Three horizons: current boss and elites left, next act, final act (A10: two bosses). `reward` prints the first two, `eval --future` all.

## 5. Plan and switch in a fight `[hyp]`
State the plan before each boss and elite (what wins it, which bucket, which condition) in the `-- why`. After turn 1-2, if the opening draw killed it, switch to the plan that uses the hand (powers bottom-decked: play 0-cost attacks to enable the three-attack relic). Solver for the micro, plan for the macro. Test: `review` shows whether the plan held.

## 6. Macro choices
- **Rest or smith: smith is the default.** A rest buys HP for the rest of this act only; an upgrade lasts the whole run, and HP comes back anyway (act transition heal, Burning Blood, Book of Five Rings, the next rest site). Rest only when the heal changes the REST OF THE ACT more than the best upgrade does over the run. Price both with `routes --hp <HP after the rest>` vs `routes` at the HP now: boss win and survival over the remaining nodes, which already count the later rests and heals. Price the best upgrade with `eval --smooth --boss --next --v "up|upgrade=ID"`; its next-act column is the long-term part. A one-fight `eval --boss` at the current HP is the wrong comparison: it ignores every heal before the boss. Rest when `routes` shows a clear survival or boss-win gain that no upgrade matches (Lagavulin: 42% at 44 HP vs 86% at 68 HP `[sim]`). With a later rest site before the boss, prefer smith now and rest there if the HP still binds. Smith the card that carries the plan (`upgrade=` variants). `[hyp]` Test: the next-act column of the smithed card vs the boss-win points the rest would have added.
- **Route:** `sts2-pathing`. **Relics and Neow:** score by the bucket filled and the route enabled. **Potions** beat cards in the same shop against hard bosses (`encounters.md`). Keep gold and potion slots for later acts once the current act is solved.

## 7. The decision record
Every pick's `-- why` carries the four fields (section 1, step 4); the `judgment:` field names the rules and blind spots that decided it and the payoff expected. After a run (`python -m agent.improve review`, tally in `evals/judgments.jsonl`): judgment held -> keep the rule and tighten its test; failed -> narrow or delete it with numbers; promote `[hyp]` to `[sim]` once measured, in the character or act skill. Open `[hyp]`: the bundle test (blind spot 1) vs smooth greedy over many sequences, and whether the elite-frequency picker can replace sim calls at pick time (`evidence.md`). Change one thing per run.
