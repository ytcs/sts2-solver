# Rebuild evidence (claims record)
Measured results behind `docs/rebuild.md`. Lower bounds under the stated solver; never verdicts on plans. Paired = same fight seeds. MxK = M candidates x K futures. Model names (h128, r1-r3, td08, gen-2, r5) label experiments; the live model is in `models/current.json`. E1-E10 data include multiplayer-only cards (E11): paired comparisons valid, levels not.

- **E1. Outcome head vs search play.** 600 fights: h128 head predicts greedy (0.664 vs 0.663) but underestimates 3x8 search (0.728, bias -0.065). -> Train the predictor on search outcomes.
- **E2. Knowledge Demon at 0%.** Silent act-2 deck 0.00; + poison engine 1.00, + power scaling 0.98, + shivs 0.22 (128 attempts). Whole-deck plans give signal where single picks are flat.
- **E3. Baseline bench (h128, 5x32 labels).** Bias eval -0.090, corpus -0.019, mix -0.126, tail -0.144. Ranking reference at 32 attempts self-agrees only 0.66: needs >= 256.
- **E4. ExIt round 1.** 88.6k fights at 3x8: bias eval -0.017, mix -0.028, tail -0.014; Brier roughly halved on every set.
- **E5. Ranking vs a 256-attempt reference (300 pairs).** Reference split-half 0.794; worth sign agreement h128 0.788, r1 0.796. One forward pass ranks deck changes like 128 paired play-outs; weakest on removals (0.57-0.63).
- **E6. Runaway step.** Real-game soft-lock (Pillage + Hellraiser + Velvet Choker, `docs/game-bugs.md` 1). Per-step guard (20,000 work units, corpus max 174) cuts it; `ov::LOOP` scores as a loss.
- **E7. Round 2.** ~157k fights: bias within +-0.011 on every set; some deciles still miss by ~0.07.
- **E8. Large search is not a better player.** 200 tail fights: 5x32/2-turn vs 8x64/3-turn (3.5x time) both 0.330.
- **E9. Distilled networks as players.** r2 policy+value: tail -0.017 +- 0.006. Softmax target over ~0.01 gaps is near-uniform; entropy 0.66 -> 1.28.
- **E10. Anchored policy target (c=2) and value-only.** No regression (tail +0.009 +- 0.005); value-only +0.012 +- 0.006. Edge of significance.
- **E11. Multiplayer-only cards.** 37 cards in ~62% of fights; flagged in `data/catalog.json` (`scripts/porting/flag_multiplayer_cards.py`), excluded from training.
- **E12. Resolution, not calibration.** r2 excess Brier 0.015-0.023 over the label-noise floor (per-fight error ~0.12-0.15) at bias <= 0.011. -> Sharpen the judge.
- **E13. Tail headroom is information.** 400 tail fights: live 0.362; clairvoyant 5x32 +0.113, 8x64 +0.120; clairvoyant policy sampling +0.019. 37.5% lost under every line.
- **E14. Round 3 pool low-signal.** 44% of fights at P >= 0.97 hold 2% of near-misses; band 0.1-0.9 holds 1/3 of fights, 73% of near-misses. -> Signal-weighted pool.
- **E15. Policy targets are mostly noise.** 2000 decisions, 5x32 twice vs 5x256: best repeats 0.663; median best-second gap 0.005, gap > 2 se in 8.5%; holdout policy loss never falls.
- **E16. Gumbel root.** No better than top-M 5x32 at any scale (default -0.0115 [-0.024, -0.001]), 150 tail states; referee best in prior top-5 91%.
- **E17. Capacity.** PPO from scratch, 1600 iterations: d128 0.664, d256 0.696; d256 led from iteration 100.
- **E18. Pool/target A/B.** Three arms on 35k fights each: all within noise (paired se 0.003-0.006). Small fine-tunes only perturb a PPO-saturated network.
- **E19. ExIt on an unsaturated network.** 198k fights: greedy win unchanged (+-0.006); outcome Brier 0.19-0.54 -> 0.022-0.037 in 3 epochs. ExIt's product is the predictor.
- **E20. Observation audit (1.25M states).** Hidden from v1: calculated card numbers (23.8% of states), selection purpose (59.4% of decisions), 13 powers' second number, rares past 16 candidates (4.2%). Fixed in observation v2 (now the only observation).
- **E21. Near-miss bench.** Replay from fight start, same fight seed, new search seed, paired with the collector: same player wins 0.335 of its own near-miss losses; paired se ~0.009. `data/bench/nearmiss.json` from r4u (1230 losses, 1500 close wins), held out.
- **E22. No policy target closes the network-search gap.** 349 significant states (5x256 reference): live 5x32 agrees 0.903; networks 0.645-0.662 under every target; 5x data 0.650. Policy-only training breaks the outcome head (Brier 0.19).
- **E23. The gap survives capacity.** 276 doubly significant states: search 0.953, networks 0.674-0.714. Sharper distilled play-out policies lose more close wins (+0.016..+0.028). -> The stack must not depend on policy quality.
- **E24. TD(lambda) value targets.** From r3, 163k fights, near-miss L->W / W->L: realized +0.011 / 0.000; TD 0.8 +0.023 / -0.014 (adopted); TD 0.9 +0.015 / -0.012. TD(0.9) best tail Brier 0.0319.
- **E25. Optimality bracket.** `tools/nearmiss_bench.py eval`, paired with r3 5x32: 5x256 honest +0.024 +- 0.014 L->W; clairvoyant +0.189 +- 0.015. 48% of near-miss losses unwinnable with perfect information; avoidable share in [~0.02, ~0.19].
- **E26. Avoidable errors are spread over setup turns.** 300 near-miss losses restarted from the true state: honest 5x256 gains +0.002 / +0.008 / +0.016 from the last / 1 / 2 turns earlier, clairvoyant +0.070 / +0.128 / +0.176. -> Sharper judge, not depth.
- **E27. TD(0.8) + cover.** Near-miss vs r3: +0.031 +- 0.007 L->W, -0.009 W->L (cover alone +0.009, TD alone +0.016). Bench tail +0.024 +- 0.007. Brier better on all sets vs r2 (tail 0.0327 vs 0.0370). Cover (every distinct legal action) adopted live.
- **E28. Decile bias (td08).** +0.027 / +0.036 / +0.027 (se 0.019-0.025) at predictions 0.27 / 0.54 / 0.81, <= 0.008 elsewhere: fails the 0.02 gate.
- **E29. PPO sampler.** d256, obs v2, 3200 iterations: p(1-p) signal sampler ahead at 27 of 32 points; greedy 12k episodes: signal 0.7142, uniform 0.7094, td08 0.6996, h128 0.6981. Signal sampler adopted; its checkpoint is the gen-2 base.
- **E30. S5: P(win run) is flat; a floored gate array stands in.**
  - Flat: 0 on every starter and recorded act-1/2 start; 512 paired rollouts separate 1 of 266 known-ranking pairs (P(clear act) 102, P(clear 2 acts) 42). Far gates are ~0 for the solver too (act-3 bosses 0/16-0/32; Knowledge Demon 0/16 in 7 of 11 decks). No potion policy lifts the next act above 0.012. Model pessimistic vs real play (act 1 cleared 0.15-0.56 vs 7 of 9 real runs).
  - Predictor blind spot: silent-poison plan deck 0.028 / 0.028 vs Knowledge Demon / Insatiable, solver 0.97 / 0.81; Accelerant removal invisible.
  - Gate array (`runmodel.gates`, 38 states): `late` (later gates floored at 0.05) sign agreement 0.91 vs P(clear act), rho 0.72 with P(win run); `mean` best on most columns (0.93, 0.81) but not a probability; unfloored product ruled by 1e-11 act-3 tails. On 93 recorded screens `price --cont late` agrees with `price` 0.81, separates 0.81, but is significantly worse on 4 of 93 (3 rests): fails the surrogate gate.
- **E31. `plans` bench slice (341 fights, td08 labels).** Bias -0.097 (plan decks -0.257); deck Spearman 0.86 all / 0.64 with >= 3 fights (r2 0.67): fails the item-3 gate. Plan-deck order right (0.87), level wrong (0.06 vs 0.32); core-card removal costs the solver 0.12, the predictor 0.02. Plan decks are out of distribution as a whole (16 cards, 1 relic, act 2).
- **E32. Gen-2 (d256, obs v2, E29 base + realized then TD(0.8) value pass).** Greedy 0.718. Near-miss with cover +0.069 +- 0.009 / -0.027 (td08 +0.032 / -0.011); bench tail +0.030. Ranks better, calibrates worse than td08. Became the live player. d256 cover at 1024 roots fills 12 GB: use 512.
- **E33. Bench relabelled by gen-2 (1505 fights).** td08 vs gen-2: Brier tail 0.0442 / 0.0492, bias mix -0.029 / -0.035, tail -0.053 / -0.059; plans bias -0.171 / -0.068, deck Spearman 0.728 / 0.747; worth Spearman 0.421 / 0.513; enabler pairs blind (0.06 / 0.13). Ranking decides: gen-2 became the predictor too.

Solver gaps: Skulking Colony #18 (forced lethal missed), Phantasmal Gardeners #1 (+0.062, se 0.005), Lagavulin Matriarch #0 (+0.16, se 0.05); K=256 search misranks order-dependent turns. - **E35. Round r5 (gen-2 + 149,830 fights, TD(0.8), policy trained).** Near-miss +0.072 +- 0.009 / -0.037 vs gen-2 +0.059 / -0.032; bench play within the replay noise floor. Brier better on 4 of 5 sets; plans bias -0.040, deck Spearman 0.768; mix -0.035, tail -0.056 (decile gate fails); enabler pairs Spearman 0.10. r5 = player and predictor.
- **E36. Exact turn search live (item 8).** Paired vs gen-2 + cover (8 attempts): +0.011 +- 0.004 L->W, -0.005 +- 0.003 W->L. Fires on ~31% of searched decisions, changes ~6%; bench wall 2.3x; worst case one decision fills the 2 s budget. Colony #18 solved; Gardeners #1, Lagavulin #0 need a horizon past the turn end.
